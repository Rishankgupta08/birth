DDear Disha,

There is something I have always found difficult to say out loud — not because I don't feel it, but because some things feel too big for words. So I built this instead. Every page of this magazine is something I have wanted to tell you for a long time.

Since you came into my life, everything has been better. You changed me. You taught me things I did not even know I needed to learn. And the way you love and care — quietly, completely — it is something I will never stop being grateful for.

This is me trying. This is me learning to show you how much you mean.

Happy Birthday, Disha.

— Rishank · Editor in Chief, Her Heart
